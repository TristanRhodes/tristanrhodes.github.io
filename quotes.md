## Quotes

A collection of quotes that speak to me.

### Simplicity

> "Simplicity is the best sophistication." - [Leonardo da Vinci](https://musequotes.com/leonardo-da-vinci/simplicity-is-the-best-sophistication-313889/)

> "Truth is ever to be found in the simplicity, and not in the multiplicity and confusion of things." - [Isaac Newton](https://musequotes.com/isaac-newton/truth-is-ever-to-be-found-in-the-453388/)

> "You cannot reduce the complexity of a given task beyond a certain point. Once you've reached that point, you can only shift the burden around." - [Larry Tesler](https://www.azquotes.com/quote/847376)

### AI / Systems

> "Once men turned their thinking over to machines in the hope that this would set them free. But that only permitted other men with machines to enslave them." - [Frank Herbert, Dune](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/842526-once-men-turned-their-thinking-over-to-machines-in-the)

> "Put a good person in a bad system and the bad system wins, no contest." - [W. Edwards Deming](https://musequotes.com/w-edwards-deming/put-a-good-person-in-a-bad-system-and-875303/)

> "AI is whatever hasn't been done yet." - [Larry Tesler](https://www.azquotes.com/quote/882543)
